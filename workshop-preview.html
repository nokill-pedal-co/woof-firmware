WOOF Workshop

Describe the pedal you want. We build the firmware, you flash it. — This is an early preview of the customer flow.

Heads up: this page is a UX prototype. The intake form is real and generated live from the WOOF hardware control schema, but the chat refinement and the “Build” step are canned mockups — there is no agent or compiler behind them yet. It’s here so we can feel the flow end-to-end.
Step 1 of 4

The vibe

One open-ended box. Tell us the sound, the band, the feeling, the use — anything. This is the only free-text input; everything else is guided so we can only build what the hardware and our vetted DSP library actually support.

Step 2 of 4

Map your controls

Every physical control on the WOOF (GuitarML FunBox v3 + Daisy Seed), one row each. Pick from the guided options — these come straight from the control schema, so each choice is something the firmware can actually do. Reserved/template-owned controls are shown read-only.

Step 4 of 4

Build & flash

When the spec is locked, we compile your firmware and hand back a flashable .bin plus a generated control page. From there it goes straight into the flasher.
