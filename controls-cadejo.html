Cadejo Control Map

A sympathetic / chordal resonator, named for the ghost dog of Central American folklore. Six tuned strings ring along with your playing — a halo, a chord, struck bells, a shimmering wall or an infinite drone. The DIPs pick the mode, the toggles set the chord and range. Click anything on the drawing.

DIP

Top view, same layout as the other canid pedals. DIPs 1-3 pick the mode (binary); DIP 4 is SUSTAIN. DIP 1 is the top switch on the right side.

    Signal path

    Your guitar excites a bank of six tuned Karplus-Strong strings. Each rings at its own pitch and decays over the time you set; a soft saturator in each string keeps the ring musical. The six are tuned to a chord (spacing) around a root, then a diffuse reverb blooms behind them.

    Modes

    DIPs 1-3 count in binary: DIP 1 = 1, DIP 2 = 2, DIP 3 = 4. DIP 4 is SUSTAIN (forces the strings to ring continuously). Every mode uses the same six strings; they differ in how the strings are tuned and excited.

    Knob pages

    Toggle 3 picks the page. Knob pickup works as on the other pedals: after a page change a knob does nothing until you sweep it past that page's stored value.
