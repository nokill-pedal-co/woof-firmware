Kelpie — Audrey II Drone

FunBox v3 + Daisy Seed · Stereo · MIDI · Expression

A pitch-tracked drone monster grafted onto the Kelpie chaos engine. Engages a free-running Schmitt oscillator that locks to the last note you played — drones indefinitely, breathes on its own, and gets bigger the more you feed it. Mixes with the guitar signal so you can play over the drone you just summoned.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level. Unity around 12 o'clock; up to ~2× past noon. Final hard-clip safety net is post-volume.
KNOB 2Fuzz3-stage cascaded clipping. Asymmetric mid stage (positive half pushed harder for even harmonics). Total gain 4× to ~600× scaling on a square curve — bottom half is usable, top half is wall-of-noise territory.
KNOB 3PitchChaos oscillator base frequency 20 Hz–4 kHz (square curve), AND drone fallback frequency when no pitch is being tracked. Also sets the divider's divide ratio (inversely — high pitch = low divisor).
KNOB 4RateLogic clock speed (1 Hz–2 kHz, square curve). Controls how often the comparator's S&H samples — at low rates you get stuttering, at high rates the gating becomes a noise carpet. Also scales the chaos oscillator 0.2×–5× when drone is OFF.
KNOB 5Bleed / Drone LevelDual-purpose: when DIP_1 is OFF, this is Bleed (cross-corruption depth between divider, oscillator, and gate — adds wobble, side-band frequencies, and unstable interactions). When DIP_1 is ON, it becomes Drone Level (0–1 mix of the drone voice with the chaos+guitar output). Internal chaos bleed is fixed at 0.3 in drone mode.
KNOB 6ThresholdSchmitt comparator trigger level (0.02–0.98). Low = comparator fires on any signal (drone-friendly). High = only loud playing fires the gate. Affects the gate's sparseness when SW2 is engaged.

3-Position Toggles

SW1 — Divide

CenterOFF. Divider bypassed. LeftSquare sub-octave. The divider counts input zero-crossings and toggles every N crossings (N set by Pitch knob, modulated by Bleed). Produces a hard square wave one or more octaves below input pitch — ring-modulates the chaos signal. RightPWM-warped sub. Same divider, but the duty cycle is modulated by the input envelope (with Bleed adding warp depth). Gives a rubbery, formant-like quality.

SW2 — Gate

CenterOFF. Signal passes ungated. LeftXOR gate. The held comparator XOR's against the divider/osc state. Hard-chopped, clicky, almost MIDI-like in feel. RightAND gate. Output only sounds when comparator AND chaos voices all align. Sparse, sputtering, much quieter — rewarding to play melodic lines through.

SW3 — Oscillator

CenterOFF in normal mode; forced ON when DIP_1 (drone) is engaged regardless of switch position. LeftSquare wave. Single-frequency Schmitt-style oscillator at Pitch × Rate freq. In drone mode, follows the pitch tracker. RightSquare + sub-square. Adds a second flip-flop running at half the osc frequency, summed in. Fatter, hollow body. Recommended for drone mode.

DIP Switches — Drone Engine

DIP 1 — Drone

Engages the Audrey II drone engine. With DIP_1 on AND Kelpie engaged (FS2):

  • The Schmitt oscillator free-runs continuously at the tracked pitch of your last played note (or the Pitch knob frequency if you haven't played yet).
  • The drone voice is mixed with the guitar+chaos output — you can still play over it.
  • Internal chaos bleed is held at fixed 0.3 to keep the engine stable while you control drone mix via the Bleed knob.
  • The drone holds indefinitely through silence — the last detected pitch persists until you play something else.
Pitch tracker: dry input is band-passed (70 Hz HP + 1.2 kHz LP), zero-crossings are detected with hysteresis (±0.02), period is converted to frequency, then smoothed with a one-pole filter and slew-limited. Tracker only updates when the envelope follower is above 0.005 — below that, the last value is held. Tracking range ~60 Hz–1500 Hz (covers full guitar range).

DIP 2 — Sub-Growl

When the drone is on, the divider's square output (one octave below tracked pitch, possibly more depending on Pitch knob) is summed into the drone voice and scaled to prevent clipping. Adds a fat, throat-y low end — the difference between Audrey as a sapling and Audrey as a giant.

DIP 3 — Breathing

A ~0.5 Hz triangle LFO modulates drone amplitude over a 0.4–1.0 range. The drone audibly inhales and exhales on its own. Pair with low Drone Level for a subtle "alive" texture; crank Drone Level for full Audrey-II-on-stage swells.

DIP 4 — Feed Me

Envelope follower on dry input drives two simultaneous modulations of the drone:

  • Gain swell: drone amplitude boosts up to 1.8× during loud playing, decays back over ~1 second of silence. Pluck harder = drone roars louder.
  • Pitch bend: the drone's frequency bends up to +5% (~+1 semitone) on each pluck, then settles. Adds an organic "responding to you" quality.

Envelope follower: fast attack (0.01), slow release (0.0008). The drone always returns to baseline because the release is gentle but inevitable.

Killer combo: DIP_1 + DIP_3 + DIP_4 together. Drone breathes baseline, and surges when you play. Crank Bleed (drone level) to 60–80%, Pitch low (around 9 o'clock for E2-ish fundamental), Threshold high so the gate only fires on intentional notes. Play sparse single notes and let Audrey react.

Expression Jack

  1. Hold FS1+FS2 for 0.5 s — enters Expression Set Mode (LEDs pulse).
  2. Move the expression pedal to your heel position. Set knobs to the values you want at heel.
  3. Move the expression pedal to your toe position. Set knobs to the values you want at toe.
  4. Hold FS1+FS2 for 0.5 s again to exit. Mappings are saved.
  5. To clear all expression mappings, hold FS1+FS2 for 2 s.

The expression pedal interpolates between heel and toe positions across all six knobs. Knobs you didn't touch in either position aren't mapped.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): bypass status — on when engaged.
  • LED 2 (right): Kelpie active. Overridden during Expression Set Mode for visual feedback.

Kelpie

Hothouse + Daisy Seed · Mono · 3-position toggles

The classic Kelpie chaos engine on Hothouse hardware: fuzz foundation with engageable logic-noise layer (ring-mod divider, XOR/bit-crush gating, Schmitt oscillator, and crosstalk). No drone mode — for that, see the FunBox variant. Footswitch convention is reversed from Terrarium per Hothouse standard: FS1 engages, FS2 bypasses.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level.
KNOB 2Fuzz2-stage cascaded tanh saturation, gain/saturation foundation layer.
KNOB 3PitchOscillator base frequency + divide ratio. Higher = faster osc, smaller divisor (less octave drop).
KNOB 4RateLogic clock speed. Slow = stuttery, fast = noise wall.
KNOB 5BleedCross-corruption depth between logic layers (osc warps divider, divider warps osc).
KNOB 6ThreshLogic gate trigger threshold (when to fire the comparator).

3-Position Toggles

TOGGLE 1 — Divide

UpRing-mod divider (normal polarity). MiddleOFF. DownInverted-polarity ring mod — same divider, sign-flipped, gives a different phase relationship with the input.

TOGGLE 2 — Gate

UpXOR logic gate. Hard chop, clicky transitions. MiddleOFF. DownBit-crush gate — sample-rate reduction in the gaps when the gate would normally drop. Creates digital granular grit.

TOGGLE 3 — Osc / Crosstalk

UpSchmitt oscillator only. MiddleOFF. DownSchmitt oscillator + crosstalk. Oscillator and divider modulate each other's frequency/ratio via Bleed.

Footswitches (note: reversed from Terrarium)

SwitchAction
FS1 (left)Kelpie engage — turns on all active toggle-switch effects.
FS2 (right)Bypass.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): Kelpie active.
  • LED 2 (right): Engaged (not bypassed).

DFU

Hold FOOTSWITCH_1 for 2 s to enter the bootloader (Hothouse built-in feature).

Fuzz Modulator

FunBox v3 + Daisy Seed · Stereo · MIDI · Expression

Granular glitch fuzz with the FunBox extras. Same 3-stage cascaded fuzz feeding a grain buffer, plus stereo grain pingpong, wave-folding, hard stutter mode, an envelope-driven Warp, and MIDI-triggered grain capture. Footswitch 2 holds for momentary delay repeats.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level. Final hard-clip safety net is post-volume.
KNOB 2Fuzz3-stage cascaded clipping with asymmetric mid stage. Total gain ~4×–600× scaling. With DIP_1 on, the final stage swaps hard clip for a sine-based wave fold (more odd harmonics, smoother fold-back).
KNOB 3ToneOnePole low-pass post-fuzz. Higher = brighter.
KNOB 4Grain SizeCapture window length, 5 ms to 200 ms. Short = hyper-stuttery; long = smearable, almost reverb-like at high rates.
KNOB 5Grain RateRetrigger frequency, 0.5 Hz to 40 Hz. With SW3 in envelope or LFO mode, this is the baseline that gets modulated ±50%.
KNOB 6WarpGrain playback speed. Knob center ≈ 1× (original speed). Half-speed at low end (deep, dragging), 2× at top end (chipmunk, glitchy fast). With DIP_4 on, envelope multiplies this by 0.5×–2× based on input dynamics. With MIDI Note On held, MIDI pitch overrides Warp.

3-Position Toggles

SW1 — Direction

CenterGrain disabled. Pedal is a straight fuzz. Useful as a baseline. LeftForward grain — captured audio plays in the original direction. RightReverse grain — captured audio plays backward. Combined with high Warp = warbly tape rewind effect.

SW2 — Feed

CenterCapture disabled. Grain buffer freezes; existing contents will play out and loop. Useful for "freezing" a captured texture. LeftClean repeats — grain captures pre-fuzz signal, plays back through the fuzz cascade. Each repeat sounds fresh. RightRe-fuzzed — grain captures post-fuzz signal, no second pass. Cleaner repeats with the original fuzz character intact.

SW3 — Modulation Source

CenterManual — knobs only. LeftEnvelope-follow — input level scales Grain Rate ±50%. Pluck harder = faster retrigger. RightLFO sweep — slow ~0.1 Hz triangle modulates Grain Rate ±50%. Hands-off slow-cycling glitch.

DIP Switches — Bonus Modes

DIP 1 — Wave Fold

Replaces the hard clip in the fuzz output stage with a sine-based wave folder (sin(in × π/2)). At high Fuzz settings produces strong odd harmonics with smooth fold-back instead of the clipper's flat-topped harshness. Pair with low Tone for a synthy, square-wave-meets-fold quality.

DIP 2 — Stutter

Skips the 1.3 ms anti-click crossfade on grain retrigger. Result: hard, clicky digital chop with no smoothing. Good for high-rate, percussive grain machine effects. Combine with very short Grain Size for granular drum-machine textures.

DIP 3 — Stereo Pingpong

Consecutive grains pan alternately to the left and right channels. The inactive channel still receives the fuzzed dry + delay signal so the pedal stays full-sounding while grains alternate. Without this DIP, both channels carry the same grain output. Especially good with envelope-modulated Grain Rate (SW3 left).

DIP 4 — Auto-Warp

Envelope follower drives Warp. Quiet input = Warp pulled down toward 0.5× of the knob value; loud input = pushed up to 2×. Plays well with light, dynamic playing — pluck softly for slow-mo grains, dig in for fast.

Killer combo: SW1 forward, SW2 clean repeats, SW3 envelope, DIP_3 + DIP_4 on. Grain Rate ~3–8 Hz, small grain size. Result is a stereo, dynamics-responsive granular cloud that gets faster and higher-pitched the harder you play.

Footswitches

SwitchActionBehavior
FS1BypassToggles on FallingEdge. Dry passes through unchanged + delay trail mixes in so repeats can decay naturally when toggled off mid-hold.
FS2Momentary RepeatsHold to engage delay feedback. Output of grain stage feeds back into the delay buffer. Release = feedback ramps back to zero.
FS1+FS20.5 s holdToggles Expression Set Mode.
FS1+FS22 s holdClears expression mappings.
FS1+FS2at power-upDFU bootloader.

MIDI

MessageMapping
CC 14–19Knobs 1–6 (with takeover — physical knob movement releases MIDI control)
CC 20Bypass toggle (≥ 64 = press)
CC 21Momentary Repeats hold (≥ 64 = on, < 64 = off)

Expression Jack

Same heel/toe learn flow as the FunBox Kelpie. Hold FS1+FS2 for 0.5 s to enter Set Mode, position the pedal at heel and adjust knobs, position at toe and adjust, exit by holding FS1+FS2 again. Hold for 2 s to clear all mappings.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): Engaged.
  • LED 2 (right): Repeats active. Overridden during Expression Set Mode.

TOGGLE 3 — Modulation Source

UpManual — knobs only. MiddleEnvelope-follow — input level scales Grain Rate ±50%. DownLFO sweep — ~0.1 Hz triangle modulates Grain Rate ±50%.

Echo Chamber

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono · SDRAM buffer

Multi-head tape delay with stretch and aging. Three read heads at different speeds (one fixed at the main delay time, two more switchable at 66% and 133% relative), reverse mode on the third head, DOD Rubberneck-style stretch, and degrading tape artifacts.

Treat Octave Fuzz

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono

Sleigh Bells / "Treats"-inspired octave destroyer. Multi-octave layering (sub, dry, octave up, +2 octaves) slammed into iMic-style ADC hard clipping for that flat-topped, harsh, digital fuzz. Lo-Fi Drop footswitch instantly degrades to phone-speaker territory.

KNOB 6Up (octave-up voice level)
iMic clipping: asymmetric hard clip with positive ceiling at 0.9 and negative floor at -1.0. Flat-topped, harsh, digital — modeled on the original iMic ADC behavior that made the "Treats" record sound the way it does.
