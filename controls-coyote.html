Coyote Control Map

Every knob, toggle, switch and footswitch on the pedal. Click anything on the drawing to see what it does. The knob labels follow toggle 3 (the page), and the per-algorithm knobs follow the DIP switches.

DIP

Top view. The DIP block sits on the right side of the enclosure; DIP 1 is the top switch here. Knobs are numbered 1–3 across the top row, 4–6 across the bottom.

    Signal path

    The same chain runs on every algorithm. The DIPs only change what's inside the generator box, and toggle 2 changes the processor.

    Knob pages

    Toggle 3 picks which six controls the knobs drive. After a page change, a knob does nothing until you turn it to (or past) where that page left it. That's the pickup, so values never jump. At power-up the page toggle 3 is on is live straight away; the other two start from the defaults shown.

    Algorithms (DIP switches)

    DIP 1 is the 1s bit, DIP 4 the 8s bit. Settings 9–15 run algorithm 0. A DIP change fades the oscillators out and back in over about 10 ms. These five knobs change meaning per algorithm; the rest don't.
