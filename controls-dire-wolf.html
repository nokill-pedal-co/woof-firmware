Dire Wolf Control Map

Sixteen fuzz circuits in one pedal: the classics, from germanium Face to Russian Muff, plus six experimental ones. The DIPs pick the circuit and toggle 1 its voicing. Page L is for playing, page C for fine-tuning, page R for utility. Click anything on the drawing.

DIP

Top view, same layout as Coyote, Dingo and Jackal. Flip the DIPs to change circuit; knobs A, B and C6 rename themselves to match. DIP 1 is the top switch on the right side.

    Signal path

    The circuit core runs at four times the sample rate (192 kHz), so the clipping stays smooth instead of fizzing with aliasing. Everything after it is shared by all sixteen circuits.

    Circuits

    DIPs 1 to 4 count in binary: DIP 1 = 1, DIP 2 = 2, DIP 3 = 4, DIP 4 = 8. Toggle 1 picks the voicing. A and B are knobs 5 and 6 on page L; C6 is knob 6 on page C.

    Knob pages

    Toggle 3 picks the page. Knob pickup works as on Coyote: after a page change a knob does nothing until you sweep it past that page's stored value. The A, B and C6 rows show what they do on the circuit you've picked above.
