Doom Engine

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono

Distortion that gets weird. Solid drive core with stackable chaos modifiers — rectify, ring mod, decimator, and resonant filter. Flip all four switches simultaneously for sonic annihilation. The Warp knob controls intensity of all active switch effects in proportion.

Knobs

KnobFunction
KNOB 1Volume (0× to 2× boost)
KNOB 2Drive (1× to 150× gain)
KNOB 3Tone (LP filter)
KNOB 4Body (pre-drive bass boost — fattens the foundation)
KNOB 5Warp (master intensity for all engaged switch effects)
KNOB 6Gate (noise gate)

Switches

SwitchFunction
SW1Rectify (octave-up fuzz character)
SW2Ring Mod (metallic dissonance, frequency modulated by Warp)
SW3Decimate (sample-rate crush)
SW4Resonance (swept bandpass filter — Warp sweeps frequency)

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass
FS2Kill switch (momentary signal cut)
Usage tip: Start with Drive (KNOB 2) around noon and Body (KNOB 4) up for a thick foundation, then bring in one switch at a time — Warp (KNOB 5) sets the intensity of every engaged effect at once, so sweep it to dial chaos in proportion. Flip all four switches together and crank Warp for full annihilation; raise Gate (KNOB 6) to tighten the noise between notes. Stab FS2 to chop the signal for stutter rhythms.

Doom Engine

WOOF v3 + Daisy Seed · Stereo · MIDI · Expression

All four effects stackable — rectify and ring mod can layer simultaneously (Hothouse's compression goes away). Stereo ring-mod detune for width, plus MIDI and expression. DIPs add stereo flavor and extra grit.

Knobs

KnobFunction
KNOB 1Volume
KNOB 2Drive (1× – 150×)
KNOB 3Tone
KNOB 4Body
KNOB 5Warp (master intensity)
KNOB 6Gate

3-Position Toggles

SW1 — Octave & Ring

CenterBoth off. LeftRectify on. RightRing Mod on.

SW1 LEFT and SW1 RIGHT can both be held simultaneously by parking the toggle precisely — but practically, use SW2 for Decimate/Resonance and combine via knob+footswitch routing.

SW2 — Decimate & Resonance

CenterBoth off. LeftDecimate on. RightResonance on.

SW3

Reserved for future expansion.

DIP Switches

DIP 1 — Stereo Ring-Mod Detune

±1.5% L/R spread on the ring modulator frequency. Creates stereo width even with the same dry mono input on both channels.

DIP 2 — Extra Grit

Pushes the asymmetric clipper's positive side 1.3× harder. More even-order harmonics, more aggressive.

FS1+FS22 s hold — clear expression mapping.
FS1+FS2at power-up — DFU bootloader.

Doom Engine

Hothouse + Daisy Seed · Mono · 3-position toggles

Bass-boosted heavy distortion (1× – 150× drive) with four stackable effects controlled by the Warp knob. Hothouse toggles compress the Terrarium's four switches — the rectify + ring-mod simultaneous stack is sacrificed, but each individually still works. Footswitches reversed from Terrarium.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level, 0–2×.
KNOB 2DriveDistortion gain, 1× – 150× (drive² × 149 + 1).
KNOB 3TonePost-distortion low-pass filter.
KNOB 4BodyPre-distortion bass boost. Adds low-end thickness before the clipper.
KNOB 5WarpMaster intensity for switch effects: ring mod freq sweep, decimate interval, resonance freq/Q/mix.
KNOB 6GateNoise gate threshold (gate² × 0.8).

3-Position Toggles

TOGGLE 1 — Rectify / Ring Mod (exclusive on Hothouse)

UpRectify on — octave-up fuzz via abs(). MiddleBoth off. DownRing Mod on — Warp-swept ring modulation.

TOGGLE 2 — Decimate

UpSample-rate crush — interval driven by Warp. Middle/DownOff.

TOGGLE 3 — Resonance

UpSwept resonant bandpass (Q 3–15, freq 200 Hz – 3 kHz exponential, mix driven by Warp). Middle/DownOff.

Footswitches (Hothouse convention)

SwitchAction
FS1 (left)Bypass toggle.
FS2 (right)Kill switch — momentary signal cut while held.
Usage tip: Set Drive (KNOB 2) around noon with Body (KNOB 4) up for a thick low end, then add one toggle at a time — Warp (KNOB 5) scales the intensity of every active effect simultaneously, so sweep it to balance the chaos. Stack Decimate (TOGGLE 2) and Resonance (TOGGLE 3) over Rectify or Ring Mod (TOGGLE 1) and ride Warp for the heaviest settings; raise Gate (KNOB 6) to tighten the note gaps. Stab FS2 (right) to kill the signal for stutter rhythms.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): Engaged.
  • LED 2 (right): Kill active.

DFU

Hold FOOTSWITCH_1 for 2 s to enter the bootloader.
