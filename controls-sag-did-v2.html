Sag-Dīd Control Map

The ritual wavefolder: pure sine folding, an envelope wah, tilt EQ and a phaser, with no clipping anywhere. Click anything on the drawing. Page L plays like the original Sag-Dīd, and every new control starts where the original's fixed setting was.

DIP

Top view. Toggle 3 is the page; page R holds the utility controls, as on Coyote, Dingo and Jackal. DIP 1 is the top switch on the right side.

    Signal path

    Your guitar (or a frozen slice of it) gets octaves added, then folds back on itself through a sine curve. Folding stacks harmonics without clipping. Your picking opens the wah, the tilt EQ leans it dark or bright, and the phaser sweeps all of it or just the lows or highs.

    Knob pages

    Toggle 3 picks the page. After a page change a knob does nothing until you sweep it past that page's stored value. At power-up the page toggle 3 is on is live straight away; the other two start from the defaults shown, which are the original's settings.

    DIP switches

    Four independent switches on the right side. They took over the original's FS2-mode toggle and its MIDI switches.
