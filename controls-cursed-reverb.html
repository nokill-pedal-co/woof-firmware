Cursed Reverb

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono

A shimmer reverb that descends into chaos. Schroeder reverb (4 comb filters + 2 allpass diffusers) with pitch-shifted feedback, saturation, and modulated delay times. Pedal-style controls for a studio-grade ambient effect.

Knobs

KnobFunctionRange
KNOB 1Volume0× to ~2×
KNOB 2DecayReverb tail length. Feedback range 0.7–0.99 — tail is always audible, won't fully die.
KNOB 3ToneOnePole damping in the feedback path. Higher = darker tail.
KNOB 4ShimmerPitch-shifted feedback amount. The tail is fed through a grain pitch shifter before re-entering the reverb — at high settings the reverb climbs in pitch indefinitely.
KNOB 5WarpDelay time modulation depth. Slow chorusing of the reverb network.
KNOB 6MixDry/wet blend.

Switches

SwitchFunction
SW1Shimmer interval (octave / fifth)
SW2Saturate feedback (Padé tanh on the feedback bus)
SW3Mod rate (slow / fast)
SW4Shimmer direction (pitch up / down)

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass
FS2Momentary freeze — input is muted, current reverb tail loops infinitely
Usage tip: For classic shimmer, push Shimmer (KNOB 4) past noon with SW1 on octave and SW4 set to pitch-up, then run Decay (KNOB 2) high for a tail that climbs endlessly. Flip SW2 to saturate the feedback so long tails stay present in the mix, or set SW4 to pitch-down for a doomy descending shimmer. Hold FS2 to freeze the current tail into an infinite pad and play over it.

FunBox v3 + Daisy Seed · True Stereo · MIDI · Expression

True-stereo shimmer reverb with per-channel comb/allpass length offsets and LFO phase offsets, plus the FunBox extras: MIDI knob takeover, learnable expression, and dual-footswitch gestures. The shimmer feedback path is mono-summed before re-injection so the stereo image stays coherent.

CenterOctave up (default). LeftFifth up. RightOctave down direction.

Center / RightOff. LeftPadé tanh saturation on feedback.

CenterSlow (default). LeftFast warble. RightOff — no mod.

Cursed Reverb

Hothouse + Daisy Seed · Mono · 3-position toggles

Schroeder reverb (4 combs + 2 allpass diffusers) with grain-based shimmer, OnePole damping, and modulated delay lines — on Hothouse hardware. Three 3-position toggles compress the Terrarium's four switches. Footswitches reversed from Terrarium per Hothouse convention.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level, scaled 2× to match bypass.
KNOB 2DecayReverb time. Feedback range 0.75 → 0.995 (long tails, near-infinite at max).
KNOB 3ToneOnePole damping filter in the comb feedback path. Lower = darker, more felt; higher = bright.
KNOB 4ShimmerPitch-shifted feedback amount. Adds the selected interval back into the reverb loop for stacking octaves/fifths.
KNOB 5WarpLFO modulation depth on delay times. Higher = pitch-warbling, chorused reverb.
KNOB 6MixDry/wet blend.

3-Position Toggles

TOGGLE 1 — Shimmer Interval & Direction

UpOctave up — classic shimmer. MiddleOctave down — sub-octave shimmer, doomy. DownFifth up — perfect-fifth interval shimmer.

TOGGLE 2 — Saturate Feedback

UpPadé tanh saturation on the feedback path — adds harmonics, helps long tails sit in the mix. Middle/DownOff — clean feedback.

TOGGLE 3 — Mod Rate

UpSlow drift — wide, lush modulation. MiddleOff — no modulation, static reverb. DownFast warble — pronounced chorus/flutter.

Footswitches (Hothouse convention — reversed from Terrarium)

SwitchAction
FS1 (left)Bypass toggle.
FS2 (right)Momentary Freeze — infinite hold, input muted while held.
Usage tip: For classic shimmer, push Shimmer (KNOB 4) past noon with TOGGLE 1 up (octave) and a high Decay for an endlessly climbing tail; TOGGLE 1 middle (octave down) gives a doomy sub-shimmer. Engage TOGGLE 2 to saturate the feedback so long tails cut through, and add Warp (KNOB 5) with TOGGLE 3 for chorused movement. Hold FS2 (right) to freeze the tail into an infinite pad and play over it.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): Engaged.
  • LED 2 (right): Freeze active.

DFU

Hold FOOTSWITCH_1 for 2 s to enter the bootloader (Hothouse built-in).
