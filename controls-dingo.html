Dingo Control Map

Every knob, toggle, switch and footswitch on the pedal. Click anything on the drawing to see what it does. The knob labels follow toggle 3 (the page). Dingo is Kelpie rebuilt: page L plays like the old Kelpie, and every new control starts where Kelpie's fixed setting was.

DIP

Top view. The DIP block sits on the right side of the enclosure; DIP 1 is the top switch here. Knobs are numbered 1–3 across the top row, 4–6 across the bottom.

    Signal path

    Your guitar is fuzzed, then a comparator turns it into logic. A slow clock samples that logic, and it gates and ring-modulates the fuzz against a frequency divider and a square oscillator, which also bleed into each other. The drone sits on top.

    Knob pages

    Toggle 3 picks which six controls the knobs drive. After a page change, a knob does nothing until you turn it to (or past) where that page left it, so values never jump. At power-up the page toggle 3 is on is live straight away; the other two start from the defaults shown, which are Kelpie's fixed settings.

    DIP switches

    Four independent switches on the right side of the enclosure. Unlike Coyote's, they aren't an algorithm number.
