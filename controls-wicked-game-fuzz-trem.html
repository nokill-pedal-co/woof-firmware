A vintage Fuzz Face that goes from a gentle hairy tear to a spitting gnarl, with a lush amp-style tremolo on top — smooth and dreamy or square-wave choppy — plus a swirling phaser after the fuzz for that wide, hypnotic Isaak shimmer.

Controls

ControlFunction
KNOB 1Output level (volume)
KNOB 2Tone (post-fuzz tilt/low-pass)
KNOB 3Fuzz drive (gain into the clipper)
KNOB 4Tremolo rate (mod_rate)
KNOB 5Tremolo depth (mod_depth)
KNOB 6Fuzz mix (dry blend back in for clarity / partial fuzz)
SW1Fuzz voicing: left = Germanium (warm, soft, saggy), center = blended, right = Silicon (brighter, harder, gnarlier)
SW2Tremolo shape: left = smooth sine, center = triangle, right = choppy square
SW3Phaser amount: left = off (bypassed), center = subtle swirl, right = deep wide sweep
FS1Bypass (fixed)
FS2Tremolo engage: short tap = latch tremolo on/off; press-and-hold = momentary (trem runs while held, off on release)
DIP1Tremolo always-on (latch trem on regardless of FS2)
DIP2unused / available
DIP3unused / available
DIP4MIDI note handling enable (off by default)
Expressionsweeps Tremolo rate (KNOB_4) heel-to-toe for live speed swells
