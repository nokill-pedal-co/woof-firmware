Sag-Dīd Ritual Wavefolder

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono

The west-coast wavefolder on Terrarium hardware: sub-octave generation, tilt EQ, an envelope filter, and a sweepable phaser, all controlled by four simple two-position switches. Fold the waveform back on itself for bright, bell-like, vowel-y harmonics, then freeze or octave-jump on the footswitch.

Signal Chain

Input → optional sub-octave (SW1) → wavefolder (Fold) → tilt-shift EQ (Tilt) → optional envelope filter (SW4, Sensitivity) → phaser (Depth/Rate, targeted by SW2) → Volume → output.

Knobs

KnobFunction
KNOB 1Volume
KNOB 2Fold (wavefold amount — reflects the waveform on itself, stacking harmonics)
KNOB 3Tilt (tilt-shift EQ — CCW dark, noon flat, CW bright)
KNOB 4Sensitivity (envelope filter responsiveness; active only when SW4 is on)
KNOB 5Phaser Depth
KNOB 6Phaser Rate
Wavefolding: unlike a clipper that flattens peaks, the folder reflects the signal back when it exceeds a threshold, adding new harmonics each time it folds. More Fold = more reflections = denser, more ring-mod-like, octave-rich timbres. It's the heart of the pedal.

Switches

SwitchFunction
SW1Sub-octave on/off (adds a sub-octave voice under the folded signal)
SW2Phaser target — full / lows only
SW3FSW2 mode — freeze / octave-up
SW4Envelope filter on/off (enables the Sensitivity-controlled auto-wah)

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass
FS2FSW2 — freeze the buffer or jump an octave per SW3.
Usage tip: SW1 on (sub) with Fold around 1–2 o'clock and Tilt just past noon gives a fat, harmonically rich lead. Flip SW4 on and add a little Sensitivity so the filter opens on hard picking. Set SW3 to freeze and hold FS2 to sustain a folded chord into a pad.

Sag-Dīd Ritual Wavefolder

FunBox v3 + Daisy Seed · Stereo · MIDI · Expression

A west-coast wavefolder: instead of clipping the signal flat, it reflects the waveform back on itself, multiplying harmonics into bright, bell-like, vowel-y timbres. Stack a sub or octave-up underneath, sweep the tilt EQ, open an envelope filter, and run the whole thing through a sweepable phaser. The FSW2 footswitch freezes the buffer or jumps an octave, and MIDI notes can drive the fold, phaser, and a glitch stutter.

Signal Chain

Input → octave/sub generation (SW1) → wavefolder (Fold) → tilt-shift EQ (Tilt) → envelope filter (Sensitivity) → phaser (Depth/Rate, targeted by SW2) → Volume → output. Folding happens before the filters so the EQ and envelope shape the harmonics the folder generates rather than feeding them in.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level. Folding adds substantial harmonic energy, so expect to pull this back from the clean unity point.
KNOB 2FoldWavefold amount. Low = a touch of edge on peaks. As you turn up, the waveform reflects more times per cycle, stacking octaves and odd harmonics into clangorous, ring-mod-like territory. The single most important knob.
KNOB 3TiltTilt-shift EQ. A single control that pivots the spectrum around a center frequency — counter-clockwise tilts toward dark/bassy (cuts highs, boosts lows), clockwise tilts toward bright/thin (boosts highs, cuts lows). Noon is flat. Tames or accentuates the fizz the folder produces.
KNOB 4SensitivityEnvelope filter responsiveness — how far and how hard the dynamic filter sweeps in response to your picking. Low = subtle wah on hard hits; high = quacky, vowel-y auto-wah on everything. Inert when the filter is bypassed (see DIP/switch notes).
KNOB 5Phaser DepthDepth of the phaser sweep — how much the all-pass notches move. At zero the phaser is effectively off; at max you get deep, hollow whooshing.
KNOB 6Phaser RateLFO speed of the phaser sweep, slow swells through fast warble.

3-Position Toggles

SW1 — Octave

CenterRaw only. The folder works on the dry input pitch alone. LeftSub + raw. Adds a sub-octave underneath the folded voice for weight and a fuller fundamental to fold against. RightSub + octave up. Sub-octave plus an octave-up voice — the widest harmonic spread, brightest and most synth-like.

SW2 — Phaser Target

CenterFull. The phaser processes the entire folded signal. LeftLows only. Phaser is band-limited to the low end — adds motion to the body while highs stay static. RightHighs only. Phaser sweeps just the top end — shimmer and sparkle motion over a solid low end.

SW3 — FSW2 Mode

CenterMomentary octave-up. Hold FS2 to jump the output an octave; releases when you let go. LeftMomentary freeze. Hold FS2 to freeze the buffer into a sustained pad; releases on let-go. RightLatching freeze. Tap FS2 to latch an infinite freeze on/off, hands-free.

DIP Switches — MIDI Note Routing

Each DIP routes incoming MIDI note pitch (and/or velocity) to a destination, so a sequencer or keyboard can play the folder dynamically. Multiple DIPs can be on at once.

DIPMapping
DIP 1MIDI note → Fold amount. Higher notes fold harder.
DIP 2MIDI note → Phaser depth.
DIP 3MIDI note → Phaser rate.
DIP 4MIDI note → Glitch stutter. Notes trigger buffer-stutter glitches for rhythmic, sequenced chaos.

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass (fires on release).
FS2FSW2 — freeze or octave-up per SW3 (momentary or latching per the chosen mode).
FS1+FS20.5 s hold — Expression Set Mode.
FS1+FS22 s hold — clear expression mappings.
FS1+FS2at power-up — DFU bootloader.

MIDI

MessageMapping
CC 14–19Knobs 1–6 (with takeover).
CC 20Bypass (≥ 64 = bypassed).
CC 21FSW2 (≥ 64 = active).
Note OnRouted to fold / phaser depth / phaser rate / glitch stutter per the enabled DIP switches.

Expression Jack

Defaults unmapped — suggested target: KNOB 2 (Fold) for foot-swept harmonic intensity, or KNOB 6 (Phaser Rate). Assign via FS1+FS2 0.5 s Set Mode.

Usage tip: for a classic west-coast lead, set SW1 to Sub+raw, Fold around 1–2 o'clock, Tilt just past noon, and a slow Phaser on Full. Pick dynamics with a touch of Sensitivity make the folder "speak." For sequenced synth lines, enable DIP 1 (note→fold) and DIP 4 (note→stutter) and drive it from a MIDI keyboard.

LEDs

  • LED 1: FSW2 active (freeze/octave-up).
  • LED 2: Engaged.

Sag-Dīd Ritual Wavefolder

Hothouse + Daisy Seed · Mono · 3-position toggles

The west-coast wavefolder on Hothouse hardware: sub/octave generation, tilt EQ, envelope filter, and a sweepable phaser, with the Terrarium's switches compressed into three 3-position toggles. No DIPs or MIDI. Footswitches are reversed from Terrarium per Hothouse convention — FS1 is FSW2, FS2 bypasses.

Signal Chain

Input → octave/sub generation (TOGGLE 1) → wavefolder (Fold) → tilt-shift EQ (Tilt) → envelope filter (Sensitivity) → phaser (Depth/Rate, targeted by TOGGLE 2) → Volume → output.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level. Pull back from unity — folding adds a lot of harmonic energy.
KNOB 2FoldWavefold amount. Reflects the waveform on itself, stacking octaves and odd harmonics into clangorous, bell-like timbres. The core control.
KNOB 3TiltTilt-shift EQ. CCW = dark/bassy, CW = bright/thin, noon = flat. Tames or accentuates the folder's fizz.
KNOB 4SensitivityEnvelope filter responsiveness to picking dynamics. Low = subtle, high = quacky auto-wah.
KNOB 5Phaser DepthDepth of the all-pass sweep. Zero ≈ phaser off; max = deep hollow whoosh.
KNOB 6Phaser RatePhaser LFO speed, slow swell to fast warble.

3-Position Toggles

TOGGLE 1 — Octave

UpSub + raw. Sub-octave underneath the folded voice. MiddleRaw only. Folder works on the dry input alone. DownSub + octave up. Widest harmonic spread, brightest and most synth-like.

TOGGLE 2 — Phaser Target

UpLows only. Phaser band-limited to the low end. MiddleFull. Phaser processes the whole folded signal. DownHighs only. Phaser sweeps just the top end.

TOGGLE 3 — FSW2 Mode

UpMomentary freeze. Hold FS1 to freeze the buffer; releases on let-go. MiddleMomentary octave-up. Hold FS1 to jump an octave; releases on let-go. DownLatching freeze. Tap FS1 to latch an infinite freeze on/off.

Footswitches (note: reversed from Terrarium)

SwitchAction
FS1 (left)FSW2 — freeze or octave-up per TOGGLE 3.
FS2 (right)Bypass.
Usage tip: TOGGLE 1 down (sub + octave up) with Fold past noon and TOGGLE 2 on Highs gives a bright, sparkling synth-fold; latch a freeze (TOGGLE 3 down) to hold a chord as a drone and play a folded lead over it.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): FSW2 active (freeze/octave-up).
  • LED 2 (right): Engaged (not bypassed).
