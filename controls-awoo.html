A.W.O.O. — Auto Wah Octave Overload

A formant filter bank and an envelope reshaper chop your signal into short, pitch-bent bark bursts, with a sub-octave for chest rumble and a glitch stutter for broken-speaker grit. Yes — it makes your guitar bark like a dog.

A.W.O.O. — Auto Wah Octave Overload

Terrarium + Daisy Seed · Mono

The bark machine on Terrarium hardware: an envelope reshaper chops your guitar into short bark bursts, a three-band formant filter gives them the nasal "woof" vowel, and each burst bends down on its tail. Sub-octave adds chest rumble and a glitch stutter breaks the bark up like a bad speaker — all on four simple two-position switches. Hold the right footswitch to howl or growl.

Signal Chain

Input → sub-octave mix (Sub) → envelope reshaper (bark bursts, Rate/Decay) → formant filter bank (~400/800/1200 Hz, shifted by Size, blended by Bark) → pitch bend (down per burst) → glitch stutter (SW4) → Volume → output.

Knobs

KnobFunction
KNOB 1Volume
KNOB 2Bark (formant filter depth — hint of growl to full bark)
KNOB 3Rate (bark burst speed — slow woofs to rapid yapping)
KNOB 4Size (dog size — left = small/yip, right = big/woof)
KNOB 5Sub (sub-octave chest rumble)
KNOB 6Decay (bark length — short ruff to long wooof)
How the bark works: the reshaper opens an instant-attack gate on each trigger and lets it decay, chopping sustained notes into punchy bursts. The formant bank — three resonant bandpass filters at the dog-bark vowel resonances — colors those bursts, and a quick downward pitch bend on each one gives the bark its falling "woof" shape.

Switches

SwitchFunction
SW1Bark style — off = single woof / on = rapid yap
SW2Trigger mode — off = pick attack / on = LFO auto-bark
SW3FSW2 mode — off = howl / on = growl
SW4Glitch stutter on/off (random tiny buffer repeats)

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass.
FS2FSW2 — howl or growl per SW3 (momentary, held).
FS1+FS2at power-up — DFU bootloader.
Usage tip: SW2 off (pick attack) with SW1 off (single woof), Bark past noon and a short Decay turns staccato picking into clipped barks. Flip SW1 on for a yap, add Sub for a bigger dog, and flip SW4 on for a broken-speaker stutter. Hold FS2 with SW3 off to howl.

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): Engaged (not bypassed).
  • LED 2 (right): FSW2 active, and blinks on each bark.

A.W.O.O. — Auto Wah Octave Overload

WOOF v3 + Daisy Seed · Stereo · MIDI · Expression

A bark machine. A.W.O.O. takes your guitar and makes it bark like a dog: an envelope reshaper chops sustained notes into short, punchy bursts, a three-band formant filter colors each burst with the nasal "woof" vowel, and every bark gets a quick downward pitch bend on its tail. A clean sub-octave adds chest rumble, the Size knob sets the breed from yappy small dog to deep big dog, and DIP-enabled glitch and crush degrade the whole thing into a broken-walkie-talkie snarl. Hold the second footswitch to howl, growl, or whimper.

Signal Chain

Input → sub-octave mix (Sub) → envelope reshaper (bark bursts, Rate/Decay) → formant filter bank (~400/800/1200 Hz, shifted by Size, blended by Bark) → pitch bend (down per burst) → glitch / crush (DIPs) → Volume → output. The reshaper gates first so the formant filters and bend shape discrete barks rather than a continuous tone.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level (0–2×).
KNOB 2BarkFormant filter depth — the dry/formant blend. Low = your guitar with just a hint of nasal coloring (a subtle growl); high = the signal is fully voiced through the formant bank for a full dog bark.
KNOB 3RateBark burst speed. Drives the envelope reshaper: the LFO auto-bark interval and the spacing of bursts inside a double/yap group. CCW = single deliberate woofs; CW = rapid yapping.
KNOB 4SizeDog size — shifts all three formant frequencies together. Left = small dog (high formants, yip); center = medium dog; right = big dog (low formants, deep woof).
KNOB 5SubSub-octave depth. Mixes a clean octave-down voice underneath the bark for chest rumble and weight.
KNOB 6DecayHow long each bark sustains before the gate closes. Short = a tight "ruff"; long = a drawn-out "wooooof". Also sets how far the pitch bend travels within a burst.

3-Position Toggles

SW1 — Bark Style

CenterDouble bark — two bursts per trigger ("ruff ruff"). LeftSingle woof — one burst per trigger. RightRapid yap — 3–4 quick barks per trigger.

SW2 — Trigger Mode

CenterLFO auto-bark — barks automatically at the Rate speed regardless of input. LeftPick attack — your picking dynamics trigger each bark. RightEnvelope follower — harder picking makes a louder, more intense bark.

SW3 — FSW2 Mode

CenterGrowl — sustained low rumble; bypasses the bark gate for a continuous menacing growl while FS2 is held. LeftHowl — sustains the signal and bends pitch slowly upward while held ("awoooo"). RightWhimper — pitch bends down, formants shift high, short soft bursts while held.

DIP Switches

DIPFunction
DIP 1Multi-bark — adds 2–3 rapid barks per trigger (excited dog). Stacks with the Rate knob and SW1 style.
DIP 2Glitch stutter — randomly repeats tiny chunks of the bark buffer at irregular intervals, like a bark through a broken speaker. Stacks with DIP 3.
DIP 3Sample crush — bit-reduces and sample-rate crushes the bark. Dog barking through a walkie-talkie. Stacks with DIP 2.
DIP 4MIDI bark — Note On triggers a bark, the note sets the formant (bark pitch), velocity sets intensity. With DIP 2 + DIP 3 on, MIDI triggers glitched, crushed barks.

Footswitches

SwitchAction
FS1Bypass (fires on release).
FS2FSW2 — howl / growl / whimper per SW3 (momentary, held).
FS1+FS20.5 s hold — Expression Set Mode.
FS1+FS22 s hold — clear expression mappings.
FS1+FS2at power-up — DFU bootloader.

MIDI

MessageMapping
CC 14–19Knobs 1–6 (with takeover).
CC 20Bypass (≥ 64 = toggle).
CC 21FSW2 (≥ 64 = active).
Note OnTriggers a bark when DIP 4 is enabled; note pitch sets the formant, velocity sets intensity.

Expression Jack

Defaults unmapped — suggested target: KNOB 4 (Size) to foot-sweep the dog from small to large, or KNOB 3 (Rate). Assign via FS1+FS2 0.5 s Set Mode.

Usage tip: for a classic bark, set SW2 to Pick attack, SW1 to Double bark, Bark past noon, Size at center, and a medium Decay — then pick staccato and it answers "ruff ruff." Turn on DIP 1 for an excited dog, or DIP 2 + DIP 3 for a junkyard-radio snarl. Hold FS2 with SW3 on Howl for a slow "awoooo."

LEDs

  • LED 1: Bypass status (engaged).
  • LED 2: FSW2 active, and blinks on each bark trigger.

A.W.O.O. — Auto Wah Octave Overload

Hothouse + Daisy Seed · Mono · 3-position toggles

The bark machine on Hothouse hardware: envelope reshaper, three-band formant filter, sub-octave rumble, and per-burst pitch bend, with the trigger and voice options compressed into three 3-position toggles. No DIPs or MIDI. Footswitches are reversed from Terrarium per Hothouse convention — FS1 is FSW2, FS2 bypasses.

Signal Chain

Input → sub-octave mix (Sub) → envelope reshaper (bark bursts, Rate/Decay) → formant filter bank (~400/800/1200 Hz, shifted by Size, blended by Bark) → pitch bend (down per burst) → Volume → output.

Knobs

KnobFunctionBehavior
KNOB 1VolumeOutput level (0–2×).
KNOB 2BarkFormant filter depth (dry/formant blend). Low = a hint of growl; high = a full dog bark.
KNOB 3RateBark burst speed — LFO auto interval and intra-group spacing. CCW = deliberate woofs; CW = rapid yapping.
KNOB 4SizeDog size — shifts the formant frequencies. Left = small dog (yip); center = medium; right = big dog (deep woof).
KNOB 5SubSub-octave depth — chest rumble under the bark.
KNOB 6DecayBark length — short "ruff" to drawn-out "wooooof".

3-Position Toggles

TOGGLE 1 — Bark Style

UpSingle woof — one burst per trigger. MiddleDouble bark — two bursts ("ruff ruff"). DownRapid yap — 3–4 quick barks per trigger.

TOGGLE 2 — Trigger Mode

UpPick attack — playing dynamics trigger barks. MiddleLFO auto-bark — barks at the Rate speed regardless of input. DownEnvelope follower — harder picking = louder, more intense bark.

TOGGLE 3 — FSW2 Mode

UpHowl — sustain + slow upward bend while FS1 is held ("awoooo"). MiddleGrowl — sustained low rumble; bypasses the bark gate while held. DownWhimper — down bend, high formants, short soft bursts while held.

Footswitches (note: reversed from Terrarium)

SwitchAction
FS1 (left)FSW2 — howl / growl / whimper per TOGGLE 3.
FS2 (right)Bypass.
FS1+FS2at power-up — DFU bootloader.
Usage tip: TOGGLE 2 on Pick attack with TOGGLE 1 on Double bark answers your picking with "ruff ruff." Roll Size left and shorten Decay for a frantic small-dog yap; roll Size right with more Sub for a big-dog woof. Hold FS1 with TOGGLE 3 on Howl for a rising "awoooo."

LEDs

  • LED 1 (left): FSW2 active, and blinks on each bark.
  • LED 2 (right): Engaged (not bypassed).
